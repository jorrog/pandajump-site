Run together, win together

Panda Jump is an endless runner for iPhone and Android. Three panda friends run in a line through a bamboo forest, and every hazard on the way needs the right panda at the front. Switch leaders in a tap and keep the team going!

🔴 HopJumps gaps, logs and thorns — and double jumps across wide rivers.
🔵 BaoRaises a shield against falling rocks, coconuts, arrows and diving eagles.
🟠 KikoThrows bamboo darts that knock down fences, boars and other foes.

Features

  • Easy mode with just Hop, and Normal mode with the whole team
  • Rescue pandas that fall behind from bamboo cages
  • Fill the golden meter for the “All together” team power
  • Snakes, eagles, monkeys, foxes, boars, rivers and crumbling bridges
  • Skins, upgrades and daily challenges, plus Game Center leaderboards on iPhone
  • No ads, no tracking
