Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Tap anywhere to use the leader’s ability: Hop jumps (tap again in the air to double jump), Bao holds up a shield while you keep your finger down, and Kiko throws a bamboo dart. Tap a portrait in the bottom-left corner to put that panda in front. Choose How to play in the menu to replay the tutorial.

A panda fell behind. Is it gone?

No! A few seconds later you will find it in a bamboo cage on the path. Run into the cage to bring it back.

What is the golden button?

It fills up as you clear hazards and collect golden shoots. When it glows, tap it for “All together”: for a few seconds the right panda handles every hazard and points count double.

What are golden shoots for?

Spend them in the Shop on upgrades (longer team power, second chance, shoot magnet) and skins. You earn them by collecting them in runs, for the distance you run, and from daily challenges.

The game has no sound.

Check the volume (and on iPhone the silent switch on the side), and that Sound and Music are On in the menu.

My scores don’t show in Game Center.

Game Center leaderboards are on iPhone. Make sure you are signed in under Settings → Game Center. Scores are sent at the end of each run.

Can I move my progress to another device?

Not yet: progress is saved on the device only and is removed if you delete the app.
