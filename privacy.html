Panda Jump

Home Support Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 October 2026

Panda Jump (“the app”) is made by Georgi Georgiev (“I”, “me”). This policy explains what happens with your information when you play. In short: I do not collect any personal data.

Information I collect

None. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not send any data to me or to third parties.

Information stored on your device

Your best scores, golden shoots, upgrades, skins, daily challenge progress and settings (sound, music, vibration) are saved only on your device. They are removed when you delete the app.

Game Center (iPhone)

On iPhone, if you are signed in to Apple Game Center, the app submits your best scores to the Panda Jump leaderboards. This is handled by Apple and is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy. I only see what Game Center shows publicly on the leaderboards. You can turn Game Center off in your device’s Settings.

Permissions

The app does not use your camera, microphone, photos, location or contacts, and never asks for them. On Android it only declares the vibration permission, used for the short buzz when a panda is hit.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
